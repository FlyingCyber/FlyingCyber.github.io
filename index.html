About Us

We bring the aviation industry's training style to cybersecurity. For cybersecurity, we focus on raising awareness within the community, predominantly in retirement communities. On the aviation side, we provide ground instruction for Private, Sport, Recreational, and Commercial certificates (still working on the certificate) that goes beyond rote memorization.

Our Mission

Our cybersecurity mission is to help people spot scams before it is too late, raise awareness of best practices, and foster a safer digital world.

Our aviation mission is to train safer pilots with stronger aeronautical decision-making and risk mitigation skills.
