Some Best Practices

  • Do not reuse passwords.
  • Do not share your passwords with anyone.
  • Avoid sponsored results when possible.
  • Be wary of scams related to pop culture events and current events
    • Sporting events
    • Concerts
    • Tax Season
    • Election Season
  • Use a password vault. The native password manager on your phone (i.e. Apple iCloud Keychain, and Google Password Manager) is better than nothing but an external subscription password manager is slightly better.
  • If you can set up your DNS (Domain Name Server) to be Cloudflare

    • Block known malicious content: 1.1.1.2
    • Block known malicious and adult content: 1.1.1.3
